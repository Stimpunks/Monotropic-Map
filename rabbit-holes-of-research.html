Rabbit Holes of Research

Flow

“Down the rabbit hole” is an English-language idiom or trope which refers to getting deep into something, or ending up somewhere strange.

A shared community coinage with no single originator.

What changes this

Being allowed to go all the way down. Depth is the point, and an environment that rewards only breadth is asking a monotropic mind to work against its own grain and calling the result a focus problem.

Where it is on the map
