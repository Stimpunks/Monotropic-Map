Privacy

This site keeps nothing about you.

No accounts. No analytics. No tracking. No cookies. No third-party anything — the fonts, the stylesheet, the scripts and the images are all served from this site, and the page is not permitted to load anything from anywhere else.

What is stored on your device

Two things, both yours, both local, and neither of them ever sent anywhere:

  • Your light or dark preference, if you use the theme button.
  • Your own marks on the map, once the marking tool ships. They stay in your browser. They are not uploaded, they are not readable by us, and clearing your browser data removes them.

There is nothing to delete on our side, because there is nothing on our side.

Sharing is always your move

If you submit a story to the community project, that goes through a form on stimpunks.org and you decide what is in it. Nothing on this site submits anything on your behalf.

Links out

We link to Autistic Realms, Stimpunks, monotropism.org, the Monotropism Questionnaire, shops, and the writers who named each area. Those are other people's sites with their own privacy practices.
