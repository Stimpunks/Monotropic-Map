Free open-source training

The Map of Monotropic Experiences: Reframing Autism Through A Neurodiversity-Affirming Lens.

Free training for professionals, families and community groups to support Autistic people. Made by Helen Edgar (Autistic Realms) with Ryan Boren, Norah Hobbs and Chelsea Adams (Stimpunks) — created by and for Autistic people.

What it covers

  • The theory of monotropism, and why flow matters so much.
  • How environments create stuck states.
  • What neuronormative domination does to Autistic wellbeing.
  • How to build flow-supportive environments where all minds can thrive.
  • Intersectionality and the double empathy problem.
  • Authentic Autistic identity, and the strength of community storytelling.

How long it takes

The content runs to about 4,000 words — roughly 45 minutes of presentation.

Delivered live with room for discussion and questions it usually runs one to two hours, and it expands into a full day if you want it to.

It suits schools, healthcare settings, workplaces and community networks.

Get it

The training pack and the workbook live at Autistic Realms, which is Helen's shop. Getting them from there is what keeps this work funded.

  • Download the training pack — a PDF with presenter notes and suggestions, a slides-only PDF, a PowerPoint, and the script.
  • Download the workbook — My Monotropic Map, a creative, neuro-affirming workbook with reflective questions and activities, for Autistic people of all ages and the people alongside them.
  • View the slides online.

It is free for individuals and for anyone with limited funds. A suggested donation of 100 US dollars, or 75 pounds, applies to organisations and professionals — the people with a training budget.
