Penguin Pebbling Cove of Friendship

Connection

In plain words

You send someone a small thing you found. A link, a photo, a funny picture. It means: I was thinking about you. It is a way of saying love.

“Penguin pebbling” is a little exchange between two people to show that they care and want to build a meaningful connection.

Helen Edgar — in their own words

What changes this

Somewhere to put the pebble, and people who know what one is. A link sent with no message is a whole sentence; an environment that reads it as noise is the thing that needs to change.

Where it is on the map

Area 2 of twenty, printed on the map as Penguin Pebbling Cove of Friendship. It borders:

  • Rhizomatic Communities
  • Forest of Joy, Awe & Wonder
  • Infodump Canyon
