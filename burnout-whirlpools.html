Burnout Whirlpools

Stuck

In plain words

You have run on empty for too long, without enough left to manage or refill. Now things you used to do are too hard. Rest on its own does not fix it. The demands have to come down.

Autistic burnout is a state of physical and mental fatigue, heightened stress, and diminished capacity to manage life skills, sensory input, and/or social interactions, which comes from years of being severely overtaxed by the strain of trying to live up to demands that are out of sync with our needs.

Dora Raymaker — in their own words

What changes this

Lowering the demands that caused it. Burnout is manufactured by years of mismatch, so it is not cured by neuronormative resilience training, and a return to the same conditions is a return to the whirlpool. The resilience that gets trained into people is compliance under another name, which is why Nick Walker, speaking at the 2026 Autistic Mental Health Conference, put it the other way round: Cultivating resilience becomes an act of defiance.
