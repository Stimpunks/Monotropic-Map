Beach of Body Doubling

Connection

A “body double” is a person or even pet who is present with us while we work. This provides a gentle form of accountability — their presence serves as a reminder of what we’re supposed to be doing so we’re less likely to get distracted.

Jessica McCabe — in their own words

What changes this

Company without supervision. The difference matters: a body double is present, not watching. The moment presence becomes monitoring it stops working and starts costing.

Where it is on the map

Area 14 of twenty, printed on the map as Beach of Body Doubling. It borders:
