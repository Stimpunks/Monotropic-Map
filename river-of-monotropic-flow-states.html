River of Monotropic Flow States

Flow

Entering flow states — or attention tunnels — is a necessary coping strategy for many of us. Flow states are the pinnacle of intrinsic motivation.

Fergus Murray — in their own words

What changes this

Let the river run. Flow is how a monotropic bodymind recharges, so time in it is not time off the work — and an environment that treats it as a reward to be earned after the real tasks has the arithmetic backwards.
