Forest of Joy, Awe and Wonder

Connection

In plain words

Happiness so big that your body has to move. Flapping, bouncing, dancing. This is stimming, and it is one of the best parts of being Autistic.

Autistic joy is one of our favorite things about being autistic. It can be intense as a meltdown, but filled with overwhelming happiness and excitement. When we experience joy, we feel the excited vibrations throughout our bodies. To release the energy, we do a “happy stim.”

Amelia Blackwater — in their own words

What changes this

Room to show it. Autistic joy is loud in the body, and an environment that requires it to be performed quietly is asking for the joy without the person.

Where it is on the map

Area 17 of twenty, printed on the map as Forest of Joy, Awe & Wonder. It borders:

  • Rhizomatic Communities
  • Penguin Pebbling Cove of Friendship
  • Campsite of Cavendish Spaces
