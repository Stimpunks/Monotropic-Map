Draw a map

The map on this site is one Autistic person's. Helen Edgar drew it in 2024 as a picture of her own monotropic bodymind, and a great many people have recognised themselves in it since.

The invitation has always been to draw your own. This is somewhere to do that.

Put an island down. Run a river through it, or don't. Make the whirlpool big enough to swallow half the coast if that is the kind of year it has been. Add the places from Helen's map that are yours, leave off the ones that are not, and add nothing at all if the shapes say it better.

Draw your own map

Put an island down, add what belongs on it, and move things until it looks like your year. Nothing you draw leaves this device, there is no score at the end, and no two maps are supposed to match.

Drag a piece, or tab to it and use the arrow keys to move it, + and − to resize, [ and ] to turn, f to flip, Delete to take it off. A river turned is a river running the other way, which is why there is one of each shape rather than eight.

There is no right map

There is no score, no type and no result here. Nothing counts what you put down or tells you what it means. The one thing this site is sure of is that a stuck state is changed by changing the conditions around a person, never by a page telling them what they are — so the tool draws, and says nothing about what it sees.

Every area on Helen's map has its own page naming who coined it and what changes it, and the marking tool asks the smaller question: where are you on her map right now. That one works with JavaScript switched off, and prints.

The workbook does all of this on paper, in more depth.

What you draw stays here

Nothing you place is sent anywhere. There is no account, no upload and nowhere for a map to go: the site has no server that accepts one, and its own security headers forbid the browser from trying.

Your map is kept in this browser, on this device, so it is still here when you come back — and it is gone the moment you clear the site's data, or press Clear the map. A different browser, or a different device, starts from blank ground.
