The twenty areas

All twenty, grouped by what kind of place they are. The grouping is Helen Edgar's own, from Stuck States vs Flow States.

Flow

Where monotropic attention does what it does well. These are not indulgences; they are how a monotropic bodymind refuels.

  • 1 Attention Tunnels named by Fergus Murray
  • 3 Tendril Theory named by Erin Human
  • 6 Rabbit Holes of Research no single originator
  • 9 River of Monotropic Flow States named by Fergus Murray
  • 12 River Banks of Monotropic Time named by Helen Edgar

Connection

Monotropic socialising, and the environments that make it possible.

  • 2 Penguin Pebbling Cove of Friendship named by Helen Edgar
  • 7 Infodump Canyon no single originator
  • 8 Rhizomatic Communities named by Helen Edgar
  • 10 Campsite of Cavendish Spaces named by Ryan Boren
  • 14 Beach of Body Doubling named by Jessica McCabe
  • 17 Forest of Joy, Awe & Wonder named by Blackwater

Stuck

States of inertia — unable to start, or unable to stop. Produced by conditions, not by character.

  • 4 Mountains of Ruminating Thoughts named by Dusty Chipura
  • 11 Meerkat Mounds named by Tanya Adkin & David Gray-Hammond
  • 15 Burnout Whirlpools named by Dora Raymaker
  • 16 Panic Hills of Low Object Permanence named by Wenn Lawson
  • 18 Lake of Limerence named by Dorothy Tennov
  • 19 Tides of the Sensory Sea named by Janae Elisabeth
  • 20 Sudden Storms of Unexpected Events named by Kieran Rose

Pressure

Not places you are, but forces acting on everything else here. These come from outside and they are made by people.

  • 5 Cyclones of Unmet Needs named by Cassidy et al.
  • 13 Shark Infested Waters of Neuronormativity and Behaviourism & Double Empathy Problems named by Damian Milton
