Cyclones of Unmet Needs

Pressure

In plain words

You asked for help and did not get it. The gap between what you need and what you are given keeps growing.

Mismatch between the areas we actually receive support, compared to the areas we would ideally like support.

Cassidy et al. — in their own words

What changes this

Meeting the need. This one is not a metaphor and it does not need reframing — it is a gap between support asked for and support given, and it closes from the side that holds the resources.

Where it is on the map

Area 5 of twenty, printed on the map as Cyclones of Unmet Needs. It borders:
